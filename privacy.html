Plain Take

What happens to your tape

Your tape is used only to write your notes. It is deleted after 7 days and is never shared.

What we keep

We keep the tape only long enough to write the one page of notes you paid for. After 7 days the tape is deleted. We do not post it, show it, or pass it to anyone else.

What the notes are

These notes are produced by an AI system and checked automatically before delivery. No person reviews them. They are not from a casting director. They do not promise a booking.

Who can buy

We do not accept a tape of anyone under 18. The link you paste at checkout should be a tape of you, an adult, that you have the right to share with us for this one purpose.

The notes page

Your notes live on a private page tied to your payment. We do not email the tape. We do not put the tape on that page.
