Framing

At three seconds the top of the head is cut off. By twenty seconds there is a wide gap above the hair, and the counter cuts across the chest.

One fix: set the laptop so the eyes sit about a third of the way down the picture, and mark that spot.

First ten seconds

A casting assistant sees a dark face, a cut-off forehead, and a scene that starts with no slate. They do not know your name yet.

One fix: slate first, in the light, with the eyes placed, before any line of the scene.
