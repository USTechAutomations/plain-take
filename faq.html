Questions about the notes

Who writes the notes?

An AI system writes them. Each set of notes is checked automatically before delivery. No person reviews them. A casting director does not write them, and the notes do not promise a booking.

What is in the notes?

One page covering framing, light, sound, slate, eyeline, pacing, choices, the three strongest moments, and the first ten seconds. Each part has one fix. See a sample written for an imaginary tape.

How fast do they arrive?

Within 24 hours after you pay $12. You paste the tape link at checkout. The notes show up on your private page. You do not need an account.

Will anyone else see my tape?

No. Your tape is used only to write your notes. It is deleted after 7 days and is never shared, posted, or shown.

Will this get me the job?

No. Nothing here promises a booking. The notes are a specific read of this one tape.

Do you take tapes of children?

No. We do not accept a tape of anyone under 18. We also do not send private messages. A public reply happens only where an adult actor has already asked in public for notes.
